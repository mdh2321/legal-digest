🇦🇺 Australia

OAIC Launches First Privacy Compliance Sweep Targeting 60 Organizations

Maddocks, Computer Weekly

Enforcement Data Privacy

The Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC) has launched its first-ever compliance sweep in the first week of January 2026. The sweep targets 60 organizations across 6 industry sectors where in-person data collection creates "power and information asymmetries" for consumers. Sectors include retailers, licensed venues, car hire companies, and real estate agents. Organizations with non-compliant privacy policies face penalties up to A$66,000.

What This Means for You

  • Review privacy policies for compliance with new OAIC enforcement standards
  • Ensure transparency in face-to-face personal information collection
  • Assess data minimization practices to avoid over-collection

Enforcement Cybersecurity

🇰🇷 South Korea

AI Framework Act Enters Into Force January 22 - World's Second Comprehensive AI Law

Future of Privacy Forum, IAPP

New Law Deadline: Jan 22 AI/ML

South Korea's AI Framework Act takes effect on January 22, 2026, making it the world's second comprehensive AI law after the EU AI Act and the first in Asia-Pacific. The Act introduces mandatory requirements for "high-impact" AI systems in critical sectors including healthcare, energy, and public services. It also establishes labeling requirements for generative AI applications. The Personal Information Protection Commission (PIPC) has developed AI-specific data processing guidelines to support implementation.

What This Means for You

  • Assess whether AI systems qualify as "high-impact" under the new framework
  • Implement labeling for generative AI features used in Korean market
  • Review PIPC AI data processing guidelines for compliance requirements

SaaS Relevance: AI-powered features deployed in Korea now subject to world's second comprehensive AI law. Immediate compliance review recommended.

🇻🇳 Vietnam

Personal Data Protection Law Now In Effect - First Dedicated Privacy Law

Rajah & Tann, Lexology | 1 January 2026

New Law Data Privacy Cross-Border

Vietnam's Personal Data Protection Law came into operation on 1 January 2026, establishing the country's first formal law dedicated to personal data protection. The law introduces fines up to 5% of revenue for cross-border data transfer violations and up to VND 3 billion for other breaches. Small enterprises and startups receive a 5-year grace period for certain obligations. Micro-enterprises and household businesses are exempt from specified requirements.

What This Means for You

  • Review cross-border data transfer mechanisms for Vietnam operations
  • Assess whether grace period exemptions apply to your organization
  • Implement consent and notice requirements for Vietnamese data subjects

SaaS Relevance: Cross-border data transfers from Vietnam now subject to formal regulation. Review data flows and transfer mechanisms.

🇯🇵 Japan

Active Cyber Defense Act Takes Effect - Critical Infrastructure Reporting Required

Nippon.com, ICLG

New Law Deadline: Nov 2026 Cybersecurity

Japan's Active Cyber Defense Act takes effect in 2026, introducing significant new cybersecurity obligations. Critical infrastructure operators must now report cyberattacks and new IT system deployments to the government. The Act establishes a Cyber Threat Information Sharing Council for public-private cooperation. An incident reporting obligation for essential infrastructure providers takes effect by November 2026. The law also grants government authority to monitor foreign internet traffic for threat detection.

What This Means for You

  • Assess whether operations qualify as critical infrastructure under the new law
  • Prepare incident reporting procedures for November 2026 deadline
  • Consider participation in Cyber Threat Information Sharing Council

SaaS Relevance: Cloud providers serving critical infrastructure may face new reporting obligations. Review customer base and prepare compliance procedures.

Home Affairs Activates Phase 2 Cybersecurity Enforcement

Enforcement Cybersecurity

This marks a transition from the initial awareness phase to active compliance monitoring and enforcement.
