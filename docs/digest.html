AI Framework Act Enters Into Force January 22 - World's Second Comprehensive AI Law

Future of Privacy Forum, IAPP | January 2026
New Law Deadline: Jan 22 AI/ML

South Korea's AI Framework Act takes effect on January 22, 2026, making it the world's second comprehensive AI law after the EU AI Act and the first in Asia-Pacific. The Act introduces mandatory requirements for "high-impact" AI systems in critical sectors including healthcare, energy, and public services. It also establishes labeling requirements for generative AI applications. The Personal Information Protection Commission (PIPC) has developed AI-specific data processing guidelines to support implementation.

Key Takeaways

  • Assess whether AI systems qualify as "high-impact" under the new framework
  • Implement labeling for generative AI features used in Korean market
  • Review PIPC AI data processing guidelines for compliance requirements

Personal Data Protection Law Now In Effect - First Dedicated Privacy Law

Rajah & Tann, Lexology | 1 January 2026
New Law Data Privacy Cross-Border

Vietnam's Personal Data Protection Law came into operation on 1 January 2026, establishing the country's first formal law dedicated to personal data protection. The law introduces fines up to 5% of revenue for cross-border data transfer violations and up to VND 3 billion for other breaches. Small enterprises and startups receive a 5-year grace period for certain obligations. Micro-enterprises and household businesses are exempt from specified requirements.

Key Takeaways

  • Review cross-border data transfer mechanisms for Vietnam operations
  • Assess whether grace period exemptions apply to your organization
  • Implement consent and notice requirements for Vietnamese data subjects

SaaS Relevance: Cross-border data transfers from Vietnam now subject to formal regulation. Review data flows and transfer mechanisms.
