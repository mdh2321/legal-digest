South Korea AI Framework Act Takes Effect January 22, 2026

South Korea AI/ML Competition

South Korea becomes the first APAC jurisdiction to implement comprehensive AI legislation. The Framework Act introduces mandatory obligations for high-impact AI systems in healthcare, energy, and public services, plus labeling requirements for generative AI content.

Key Takeaways

  • High-impact AI systems in 11 specified areas face new compliance requirements including impact assessments, risk management, and human oversight
  • Mandatory watermarking and labeling required for AI-generated content - assess product implications
  • Foreign companies must appoint a domestic representative in Korea if no local presence

First comprehensive AI law in APAC creates compliance obligations for any AI-powered features used by Korean customers. Penalties up to KRW 30 million (~$20,870) and potential imprisonment for violations.
