🇰🇷 South Korea

AI Basic Act Takes Effect January 22 - World's Second Comprehensive AI Law

BABL AI, Future of Privacy Forum, IAPP

New Law Deadline: Jan 22 AI

South Korea's AI Basic Act takes effect January 22, 2026 - just 2 days away - making it the world's second comprehensive AI law after the EU AI Act and the first in Asia-Pacific. The Act introduces mandatory requirements for "high-impact" AI systems in critical sectors including healthcare, energy, and public services. Key obligations include user notification of AI use, impact assessments for high-impact AI, risk management systems with human oversight, mandatory watermarking for AI-generated content, and appointment of a domestic representative for foreign companies. Industry receives a one-year grace period focused on guidance rather than penalties.

What This Means for You

  • Assess whether AI systems qualify as "high-impact" under the new framework by January 22
  • Appoint a domestic representative if operating AI systems in Korea without a local address
  • Implement watermarking for generative AI content and user notification systems

🇭🇰 Hong Kong

First Cybersecurity Law Now in Effect - Critical Infrastructure Operators Face New Obligations

Baker McKenzie, China Briefing

New Law Cybersecurity Critical Infrastructure

Hong Kong's Protection of Critical Infrastructures (Computer Systems) Ordinance took effect January 1, 2026 - the city's first dedicated cybersecurity law. The law targets 8 critical sectors: energy, IT, banking and financial services, air transport, land transport, maritime transport, healthcare, and telecommunications. Banking and financial services are designated as Category 1 Critical Infrastructure with heightened obligations including mandatory computer system security management units and participation in security drills every two years. Non-compliance may result in significant penalties.

What This Means for You

  • Assess whether operations fall within the 8 designated critical infrastructure sectors
  • Establish dedicated computer system security management units if designated
  • Prepare for mandatory security drill participation as required by the Commissioner

🇸🇬 Singapore

IMDA Digital Communications Consultation Closes January 21

OpenGov Asia, IMDA

Deadline: Jan 21 Telecoms Platform Regulation

Singapore's Ministry of Digital Development and Information and IMDA launched a public consultation on proposed amendments to the Info-communications Media Development Authority Act, closing January 21, 2026. The amendments address the convergence of telecommunications, digital platforms, and media services. Key changes include requiring IMDA approval for transactions giving 30%+ voting power in regulated entities. Pro forma transactions that don't change voting power will need notification only. A new Online Safety Commission is expected to be established by H1 2026 to address 13 categories of online harms.

What This Means for You

  • Submit consultation feedback by January 21 if operating regulated services in Singapore
  • Review ownership structure for IMDA approval requirements under new thresholds
  • Prepare for new Online Safety Commission framework expected H1 2026

🇯🇵 Japan

Active Cyber Defense Law and New Five-Year Strategy Take Effect

Center for Cybersecurity Policy, The Cyber Express

New Law Cybersecurity Deadline: Nov 2026

Japan's Active Cyber Defense Law has passed, marking a pivotal shift in national cybersecurity strategy. The law grants government authority to monitor foreign internet traffic traversing domestic infrastructure for threat detection. The government also adopted a new five-year Cybersecurity Strategy (2025-2030) explicitly identifying cyber operations from China, Russia, and North Korea as "serious threats." The Cybersecurity Council will be re-established by November 2026 with expanded involvement of the Prime Minister, essential infrastructure providers, and IT vendors. Incident reporting obligations for critical infrastructure take effect by the same deadline.

What This Means for You

  • Assess critical infrastructure designation under the new cybersecurity framework
  • Prepare incident reporting procedures for November 2026 deadline
  • Review data flows through Japan infrastructure given new monitoring authorities

🇦🇺 Australia

OAIC Privacy Compliance Sweep Underway - 60 Organizations Targeted

National Law Review, OAIC

Enforcement Data Privacy

Australia's Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC) is conducting its first-ever compliance sweep in January 2026. The sweep targets approximately 60 entities from sectors including pawnbrokers and second-hand dealers where in-person data collection creates power and information asymmetries. Non-compliant privacy policies may result in penalties up to AUS$66,000. Looking ahead, the Children's Online Privacy Code draft will be released for consultation in early 2026, with final implementation by December 10, 2026. Automated decision-making (ADM) disclosure requirements in privacy policies take effect December 11, 2026.

What This Means for You

  • Review privacy policies for compliance with Australian Privacy Principles ahead of sweep
  • Prepare for Children's Online Privacy Code requirements if handling children's data
  • Update privacy policies to disclose automated decision-making by December 2026

🇻🇳 Vietnam

Digital Technology Industry Law Now in Effect - Regulatory Sandbox for Virtual Assets

Vietnam Briefing, Nation Thailand

New Law Fintech Digital Assets

Vietnam's Digital Technology Industry Law came into force January 1, 2026, passed with 441 out of 445 National Assembly votes. A landmark feature is the establishment of a regulatory sandbox allowing businesses to test virtual asset technologies under state-approved conditions with temporary exemption from certain legal obligations. For the first time, Vietnam has introduced a legal framework for digital assets including crypto assets, with strict regulation for cybersecurity and anti-money laundering. The law positions Vietnam as a leader in Southeast Asian digital economy governance.

What This Means for You

  • Explore regulatory sandbox participation if testing virtual asset products in Vietnam
  • Review crypto/digital asset offerings for compliance with new legal framework
  • Ensure AML/KYC procedures align with Vietnam's digital asset requirements

🌎 ASEAN

Digital Economy Framework Agreement Substantially Concluded - World's First Regional Digital Pact

World Economic Forum, CIGI

New Agreement Cross-Border AI

ASEAN has reached "substantial conclusion" in negotiations for the Digital Economy Framework Agreement (DEFA), to be signed in 2026. This will be the world's first region-wide agreement focused exclusively on digital economy governance. DEFA aims to harmonize digital trade rules, enable trusted cross-border data flows, and establish coherent regulations for e-commerce, cybersecurity, and digital payments across ASEAN's 680 million-person market. The digital economy could reach $2 trillion by 2030. A unified digital customs system and interoperable e-invoicing could cut cross-border transaction costs by 30%.

What This Means for You

  • Monitor DEFA signing for opportunities to streamline cross-border data transfers in ASEAN
  • Plan for harmonized e-commerce and digital payment regulations across the region
  • Engage with industry associations on DEFA implementation guidance as it develops
